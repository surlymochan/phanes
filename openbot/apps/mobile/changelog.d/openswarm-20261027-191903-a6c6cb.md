### Added

- The text of a chat composer stays on the phone. A chat that closes and opens again has the text
  you typed, also after the app restarts.
- The text of a chat that the phone could not save is written again, so it stays after a restart.

### Fixed

- The text of a chat composer that you cleared or sent while the phone read its saved text came
  back. It stays away now.
- The text of a chat composer is written when the app goes to the background. Before, the phone
  could close the app before the text reached the file.
